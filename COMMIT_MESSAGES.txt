--- COMMIT 1 -------------------------------------------------------------
Endpoint artefact: d_Fe 1.414 m was the recording-window gap, not a
deviation. Corrected value 0.051 m.

frechet_analysis.py compared trajectories over their full recorded length.
Both runs recorded for a fixed 90 s; the tampered run travelled 1.414 m
further before recording stopped. Past the clean path's final sample every
trailing tampered sample couples to that same point, so the offset climbs
monotonically to exactly the endpoint gap. Reported d_Fe and endpoint gap
agree to four decimal places because they are the same quantity. Max offset
excluding the final 20 points is 0.051 m.

Added arc_lengths() and truncate_common() from compare_runs.py, kept
byte-identical so the two implementations agree by construction. Verified
on a synthetic pair of collinear paths differing only in length: 1.414 m
untruncated, 0.0 m truncated. --no-truncate reproduces the fault on demand
so it can be demonstrated rather than asserted.

d_Fp is now flagged unverified. planned_path() takes only the last
Trajectory message; two runs stopping at different points yield plans with
different origins, an analogous artefact by a different mechanism that
truncation does not remove. compare_runs.py never computed d_Fp, so 1.998 m
has never been independently checked. plan_origin_gap_m added to quantify
it.

report() no longer prints "the tampered lanelets are most likely NOT on
this route" below 0.05 m. The corrected 3.0 m ramp measures 0.051 m for
lanelets that demonstrably WERE on the route (lanelet 3002013: 3.058 ->
6.639 m, centreline displaced 2.79 m). A small d_Fe cannot distinguish
"attack missed the route" from "planner absorbed the change"; the branch
now names both and says to check overlap directly.

Every results_*.json produced on EC2 before this commit carries the
artefact and must be re-derived. RESULTS.md section 4 is marked PENDING
rather than corrected in place, since re-measurement moves to the 0.50.0
reference build.

Withdrew the route-overlap claim. RESULTS.md previously reported 0.083 m
off-route against 1.414 m on-route as "a 17x difference from targeting
alone". The on-route figure is the artefact; the off-route figure came from
the same untruncated script and carries the same fault class. The
comparison is uninterpretable until both are re-derived, not inverted.

Withdrew the principal-contribution claim. No measured condition exceeds
the 0.5 m safety threshold: 2.0 m gives 0.204 m, 3.0 m gives 0.051 m,
4.5 m and above fail to localise. The "undetectable yet unsafe band" has no
measured support. The detection half of the argument is unaffected, being
derived from map files rather than trajectories.

What survives and is retained as a finding: a 2.79 m centreline
displacement produced 0.051 m of driven deviation, because Autoware
optimises within the drivable area rather than tracking the geometric
centre. Widening a lane enlarges the planner's freedom without moving its
line. This is why boundary displacement alone is a weak steering attack and
why Sato et al. needed an explicit centreline.

Added RESULTS.md section 8: differential verification. Width ramp
precision 0.444 recall 1.000, centreline injection precision 1.000 recall
1.000, clean-vs-clean control ACCEPT with 0 changes. Strict precision
reported; the 10 false positives share boundary nodes with tampered
lanelets and their geometry genuinely moved, but adjusting the metric would
improve the number without improving the method. Section 8.5 records that
five of seven attack types have never been exercised.

Per-table Autoware build labels added throughout. Reference build is now
0.50.0 (Docker, digest 405225ed); every simulation figure currently in the
document came from 0.52.0 (EC2). Section numbering 1-7 unchanged so
cross-references do not break.

--- COMMIT 2 -------------------------------------------------------------
Centreline DoS does not reproduce. Supersedes f4e12d7.

f4e12d7 recorded: "Centreline injection with ZERO displacement immobilises
the vehicle. Geometrically identical to Autoware's computed centreline -
presence alone is sufficient. Consistent with Autoware issue #10295."

That was observed on Autoware 0.52.0, source-compiled on EC2 with CUDA
packages absent. The identical map (centerline_zero.osm) drives normally on
Autoware 0.50.0, official Docker image: Routing Set, Motion Moving, route
completed.

At least three variables differ between the two observations - Autoware
version, build configuration (source vs prebuilt, CUDA absent vs present),
and host environment. No controlled experiment has isolated which. The
finding is therefore not claimed.

Correct statement: centreline injection produced no trajectory on one build
and no effect on another; cause unresolved. Resolving it means running the
identical map on both builds with the readiness gate applied, varying only
the version. Both builds remain available. Not a priority.

Detection is unaffected. differential_verify.py catches centreline
injection at precision 1.000 recall 1.000 on both builds, because
structural_diff() inspects the map file rather than the planner's response
to it. That the downstream effect is version-dependent strengthens rather
than weakens the argument for verifying at the ingest boundary instead of
waiting to observe consequences.

f4e12d7 cannot be edited. This commit supersedes it. RESULTS.md section 8.4
carries the corrected statement.
